## Ticketing Dashboard

The NorthStar ticketing simulator tracks three fictional identity lifecycle requests:

- **Joiner:** Onboard Sarah Johnson as a Talent Acquisition Specialist.
- **Mover:** Transfer James Whitfield from HR to Finance.
- **Leaver:** Remove Lisa Park’s access following her departure.

![NorthStar JML ticket dashboard](screenshots/01-northstar-jml-ticket-dashboard.png)

*All three requests are open and awaiting simulated approval. This dashboard is a training simulator, not a live ServiceNow instance.*
